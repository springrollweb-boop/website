Our story

A simple idea, served generously

Spring Roll Station was created with a simple idea: to make well loved Vietnamese food easy and enjoyable for everyone in Singapore, with a strong focus on quality ingredients, generous portions and honest value.

From fresh spring rolls and crispy bánh mì to comforting bowls of phở and other Vietnamese favourites, we take pride in serving satisfying portions without compromising on the quality of our ingredients or the flavours that go into every dish.

What started at our original Upper Cross Street outlet has grown together with the support of our regular customers. In 2026, we relocated this much loved outlet to Novena Regency, bringing our food to a new neighbourhood while continuing to welcome the customers who have supported us over the years.

At Skysuites at Anson in Tanjong Pagar, we have also introduced a more modern and comfortable casual cafe style dining experience. It is a relaxed place to enjoy Vietnamese food, coffee and drinks throughout the day.

What makes us different

Quality food, ample portions, warm hospitality

As Spring Roll Station continues to grow, our philosophy stays the same: approachable Vietnamese flavours at prices that offer genuine value.

01

Vietnamese at heart, made for Singapore

We stay true to the flavours and spirit of Vietnamese food while understanding the local palate. Our menu balances authenticity with dishes that are approachable and enjoyable for customers in Singapore.

02

Quality food and generous portions

A satisfying meal should be both good and generous. From our signature phở and bánh mì to fresh spring rolls, we focus on quality ingredients, ample portions and great value.

Traditional favourites, modern ideas

We respect the classics, but we are not afraid to have fun and evolve. Alongside familiar favourites, we explore new ingredients, combinations and modern dishes, always with a Vietnamese touch.

Signature phở, bánh mì, fresh spring rolls, rice and noodle dishes, Vietnamese coffee, drinks and more. We keep introducing new choices while keeping our most loved favourites.

Spring Roll Station is warm, casual and a little playful. We keep up with changing tastes, listen to our customers and look for fresh ways to make Vietnamese food exciting while staying connected to its roots.

Our promise

Whether it is a quick bánh mì and coffee, a comforting bowl of phở, or a relaxed meal with family and friends, we want our customers to leave feeling satisfied and wanting to come back.
