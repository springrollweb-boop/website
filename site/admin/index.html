    Add-on groups

    Groups of choices shown in the popup when a customer taps an item. Attach them to items from the item form.

    How this works

    • Sections are the categories on the menu page (Phở, Bánh Mì and so on). Use the arrows to change their order. A hidden section disappears from the website.
    • Items belong to a section. Each has a name, an optional Vietnamese name, a price, a description, a photo and badges such as Best seller or New.
    • Sold out keeps the item on the menu but greys it out so customers cannot add it to an order. Hidden removes it from the website entirely.
    • Add-on groups are reusable lists of choices (phở add-ons, sauces, meat choice). Tick the groups an item should offer in its form. A "pick one" group makes the customer choose; a "pick any" group is optional extras.
    • Photos are uploaded to the database storage and shown immediately. Square photos around 900 by 900 pixels look best.
    • Changes are live on the website as soon as you save. There is no publish step.
